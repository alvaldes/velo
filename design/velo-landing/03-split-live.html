velo github ↗

Your music, in the terminal you already have.

One Rust binary indexes your folder from its own tags, plays it gaplessly in the window you are already in, and draws the cover art where the terminal can show it.

  • no daemon
  • no server
  • no account
  • no telemetry
Install — pick your platform
curl -L github.com/alvaldes/velo/releases/latest/download/velo-aarch64-apple-darwin.tar.gz | tar -xz -C ~/.local/bin

Puts velo in ~/.local/bin — make sure it is on your PATH. Every archive is built by CI and paired with a published checksum.

What is in the box

  1. 01Gapless, and honest about it

    ReplayGain from the files' tags, a bit-perfect mode that reopens the device at the file's own rate, and a mono downmix that refuses under bit-perfect instead of quietly resampling.

  2. 02Four views over your own tags

    Artists, albums, tracks, genres — indexed from the files, refreshed in the background at every launch, with fuzzy search across all of it and a queue you edit with tab.

  3. 03Radio that reads like radio

    Favourites, pinned places and a ● live row with the ICY title; a station that cannot play says so at once rather than waiting for a timeout.

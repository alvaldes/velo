Your music, in the terminal you already have open.

velo reads your files' own tags, plays them gaplessly and draws the cover art. It is one Rust binary: no daemon, no server, no account, no telemetry.

Install on
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alvaldes/velo/main/install.sh | sh

The script picks the binary for this machine, checks it against the .sha256 CI published beside it, and puts velo in ~/.local/bin. No sudo.

It plays the file, not an approximation of it

Gapless
A join trims what the encoder inserted, so the seam carries music only: the LAME delay and padding for MP3, the edit list for M4A.
ReplayGain
Read from the files' own tags and applied with clipping protection. velo can also measure an album the EBU R128 way and write true-peak tags, and only when you add --apply.
Bit-perfect
bit_perfect = true reopens the device at each track's own rate. ReplayGain and the volume step aside, and one status line says so.
Formats
MP3, FLAC, M4A/AAC, OGG Vorbis, OGG Opus and WAV, with no dynamic dependency.

Your folder is the library

Four views
Artists, albums, tracks and genres, indexed from the tags and refreshed in the background at every launch.
Fuzzy search
/ searches the whole library: lofi finds Lo-Fi Scientists.
A queue you edit
a appends without stopping playback, tab goes to the queue and back, z shuffles what is left.
Favourites and playlists
f marks a favourite and S saves the queue as a playlist. .m3u, .m3u8 and .pls files import and export from the shell.
Recently played
velo history --json hands your last 500 plays to a script.

Radio that reads like radio

58,000 stations in the Radio Browser directory

241 countries, with yours listed first

  • Browse by country, region or tag. f favourites a station or pins a place.
  • A live row carries ● live and the listener count when the service reports one.
  • u plays any stream URL without saving it.
  • A station that cannot play is marked ✗ and says why at once, and i drafts the bug report for you to send.

It looks like your terminal

Your colours
Seven theme colours in config.toml, or the terminal's own background and text. transparent_background = true lets whatever is behind the window show through, which is what this page is doing.
Nine visualizers
bars, bars-outline, wave, scatter, flame, matrix, scope, mirror and none. v cycles them and V gives one the whole screen.
Cover art
Drawn where the terminal speaks the kitty, sixel or iTerm2 protocol. Elsewhere the status line says why it is hidden.
Low power
P redraws the screen at a quarter of the rate and leaves the spectrum out. Playback does not change.

Your own server

Early access: velo talks to servers that expose the Subsonic API, Navidrome, Airsonic and Gonic among them. velo --sync brings the server's library into the index, and a remote track plays through a cache.

What velo sends

No usage data: no analytics endpoint, no install id, no first-run question. One request is its own, the update check to api.github.com, once a day with no identifier, and check = "never" turns it off.

Not yet

Windows is untested. Subsonic cannot stream a track before the whole file is cached. Playlists are saved, renamed and reordered, and have no editor beyond that.

Three lines to the first song

Linux or macOS. Building from source needs Rust 1.88 or newer.
